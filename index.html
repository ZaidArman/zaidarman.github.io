03

MIMS Medicine Inventory Management System

Live Python · Flask · SQLAlchemy · React · MySQL · AI/ML

A healthcare inventory product for pharmacy operations from stock visibility to inventory intelligence: batch tracking, expiry management, dispensing workflows, and AI demand forecasting for procurement.

Problem

Pharmacies manage thousands of SKUs where the failure modes are specific and expensive: expired stock is a write-off and a compliance risk, stock-outs send patients elsewhere, and manual batch tracking makes both routine.

Engineering

I designed the backend around batch-level inventory as the core abstraction every unit of stock belongs to a batch with its own expiry and supplier lineage, so intake, storage, dispensing, and reconciliation stay consistent by construction. Flask REST APIs expose workflow-shaped operations rather than raw tables; the relational schema keeps batch, expiry, and supplier data in one consistency boundary; and the ML pipeline turns sales history into demand forecasts that surface as reorder recommendations, not raw numbers.

CERS Consumer Electronics Retail System

Enterprise ERP · In development Python · Django · DRF · React · PostgreSQL

An ERP platform that runs an electronics retail business end to end built around the reality that retailers track serialized items, quantity stock, and batch stock simultaneously, across branches, and the books have to agree.

Products→ Inventory→ Suppliers→ Purchasing→ POS / Sales→ Payments / Accounting→ Analytics / Reporting

CROSS-CUTTING Granular RBAC · Multi-branch workflows · Serialized stock · Quantity stock · Batch stock

Engineering

The interesting problem is the inventory model: an individual phone tracked by IMEI, a box of cables tracked by quantity, and batch-tracked accessories are different shapes of the same concept. The schema models all three modes without duplicating logic, so stock intake, transfers, sales, and reporting share one code path. APIs are organised around business workflows rather than raw tables, granular role-based access control scopes every operation, and the multi-branch design keeps stock, sales, and reporting branch-aware end to end.
